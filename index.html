Common Questions

Straight answers to what people ask most after a car accident.

How much does it cost to hire a lawyer?

Nothing up front. We work on a contingency fee, which means we're only paid if we recover money for you.

Should I talk to the other driver's insurance company?

It's best to speak with a lawyer first. Adjusters may use what you say to reduce or deny your claim.

How long do I have to file a claim?

Every state sets a deadline, called the statute of limitations. Missing it can mean losing your right to compensation, so it's important to act quickly.

What if I was partly at fault?

You may still be able to recover compensation, depending on your state's laws. A free case review can help you understand where you stand.
